# Review Queue — Genetics / Aneuploidy Q11–Q25

Source: Pediatric Saudi Board Exams Question Collection, 4th Edition, 1/May/2026.

- **Q11 Trisomy 18:** original dysmorphology image required.
- **Q13 Down syndrome 14;21 translocation:** karyotype image required. Source option says paternal chromosome study, while the explanation recommends chromosome analysis of **both parents**.
- **Q14 recurrence risk:** source gives 10–15% for a maternal 14;21 Robertsonian translocation carrier; independently verify counseling figures.
- **Q17 Turner syndrome:** karyotype image required.
- **Q18 Turner vs Noonan:** source favors Turner from female short stature + repaired coarctation, but phenotype is limited; retain `needs_verification`.
- **Q19 suspected Noonan:** source explicitly states **none of the listed options is correct**. Keep `conflicting` and do not force a key.
- **Q20 Klinefelter:** karyotype image required.
- **Q22 Klinefelter complication:** source key is breast cancer, but osteoporosis is also a recognized complication; options may contain more than one true statement.
- **Q23 Tanner stage 1 at age 12:** source itself notes this does not necessarily satisfy delayed-puberty criteria; keep `incomplete_recall`.
- **Q24 elevated maternal AFP:** source key is "neural tube defect," but "anencephaly" is itself a neural tube defect, so the answer choices overlap. Keep `conflicting`.
- **Q25 genetic counseling:** source explicitly says maternal age >35 is most likely, **or paternal age >40 may also be correct**. Keep `conflicting` until the intended question is recovered.

Publication rule: no image-dependent or structurally ambiguous aneuploidy question advances to `ready_for_publish` until the source image/options are reconciled.
