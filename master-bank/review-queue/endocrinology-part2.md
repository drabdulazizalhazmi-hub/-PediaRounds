## High-priority verification/conflicts
- Q2 delayed puberty: source itself says the information is insufficient; Tanner I at age 14 generally warrants evaluation unless CDGP has already been established.
- Q7 low IGF-1: do not start growth hormone solely from low IGF-1. Confirm the GH-axis diagnosis and exclude alternative causes.
- Q15 McCune-Albright: source key says GnRH agonist, but classic McCune-Albright precocious puberty is gonadotropin-independent; GnRH agonists are not primary therapy unless secondary central activation occurs.
- Q28 rickets initial laboratory test: source favors calcium but simultaneously quotes a multi-test initial evaluation. Keep as `conflicting` until a clean modern stem/answer is chosen.
- Q31 PPI-related hypomagnesemia: mechanism is sound, but a two-month exposure is shorter than typical chronic PPI case reports; keep the Crohn/malabsorption contribution explicit.
- Q42 46,XY DSD: one original option was incompletely recalled; do not publish until original choices/hormone details are reconstructed.
- Q54 pediatric diabetes phenotype: obesity/acanthosis favor type 2 diabetes, but glucose 500 mg/dL does not exclude type 1; evaluate ketosis and diabetes autoantibodies/C-peptide as appropriate.
- Q55 prediabetes: 2-hour OGTT 160 mg/dL clearly indicates impaired glucose tolerance. Fasting 100 mg/dL qualifies as IFG under ADA thresholds but not under every historical standard; label the diagnostic standard.
- Q58 DKA shock bolus: use current pediatric DKA/ISPAD wording and reassessment; the source recall selects 20 mL/kg.
- Q59 DKA glucose ~70 during treatment: exact action depends on current/local DKA protocol; increase dextrose and adjust insulin only as needed while continuing ketone clearance.
- Q61 DKA cerebral injury: osmotic shift is a historical explanation, not a complete modern causal model. Keep `outdated` note visible.
