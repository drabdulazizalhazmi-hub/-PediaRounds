# Review Queue — Infectious Diseases / Infections Caused by Atypical Bacteria Q61–Q75

Source: Pediatric Saudi Board Exams Question Collection, 4th Edition, 1 May 2026.

## Must review before publish

- **Q61 — PPD 12 mm in healthy 6-year-old, no risk factors:** source preserves `A. Reassurance` but explicitly documents conflicting TST-threshold interpretations and says the PPD result alone is insufficient to start treatment. Status: `conflicting`.
- **Q62 — PPD positivity at 6 mm in child on chemotherapy:** source answer B is preserved. Thresholds are guideline-sensitive and should be checked against current TB guidance before publication.
- **Q63 — 'positive' PPD 11 mm + normal CXR = TB infection:** conceptually fits TB infection if the PPD is truly positive, but the source itself says 11 mm may not be positive in this low-risk child. Status: `conflicting`.
- **Q64 — TB contact + positive TST, next test:** source answer is sputum/gastric aspirate, while its own explanation places chest radiography before microbiologic specimens unless symptoms/imaging are abnormal. Stem lacks CXR context. Status: `incomplete_recall`.
- **Q66 — Mycoplasma + erythema multiforme:** original targetoid-lesion image is required. Status: `image_missing`.
- **Q67 — Atypical pneumonia:** original chest X-ray is required. Status: `image_missing`.
- **Q70 — isolated infant axillary swelling:** source explicitly says there is not enough information, especially size/suppuration. No answer should be forced. Status: `incomplete_recall`.
- **Q71 — BCG axillary lymphadenitis:** source answer A applies only if nonsuppurative; original lesion image is required to confirm this. Status: `image_missing`.
