- **Q12 — anti-NMDA receptor encephalitis:** EEG image/extreme delta brush is central to the question; original image required.
- **Q13–Q14 — absence epilepsy:** EEG image is central; original image required.
- **Q15 — benign sleep myoclonus:** age/timing wording is potentially confusing with infantile spasms; verify source recall and current diagnostic framing.

- **Q18 — infantile spasms treatment:** source selects vigabatrin despite no tuberous-sclerosis clue in the recalled stem; compare against current first-line guidance before accepting the answer.
